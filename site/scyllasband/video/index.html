Scylla’s Band

The launch video

Four voices joke their way through launch day in six languages, then the band sings, all fourteen of them by the last chorus. Every voice and every sung note in it is Scylla’s Band; the facts drop in underneath as they go.

What you’re watching

  • The voices. Scylla, Ariadne, Orpheus and Thistle talk, Tuesday raps, and all fourteen sing the last chorus. Each voice is an orb that swells with its own loudness and splashes on its consonants.
  • The timing. Every word lands on screen when the model’s own phone durations say it’s spoken. Sung lines scroll past at the pitch of each note in the score.
  • The receipts. “Made in 26 ms” under a line is how long that line took to render, warm, on a MacBook Pro with an M5 Max (Core ML bundle, GPU).
  • The facts. Each figure comes from the README, the model card or the open-tts-leaderboard evaluation of release v4-20261106.

How it was made

The video is a program. A script and melody scores go in; the scyllasband runtime renders every line and every note; Whisper checks that each line says what it should; and the model’s timings, pitch and loudness drive the picture, drawn in code as one field of characters with Remotion and a WebGL shader. Change a line and the video follows.

The instrumental (drums, bass, keys and synth) was generated in ACE Studio around the band’s finished vocals. ACE Studio didn’t touch the voices.
