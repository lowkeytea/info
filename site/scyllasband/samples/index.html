Face Down

Ink · Poppy · Gwen · Felix · Priya · 232 words · 10 cues, 7 sung

A 120 bpm pop song about a night with the phone face down on the table. Poppy sings the first verse, Gwen the second, and the chorus is Poppy with Gwen doubling and Felix an octave below; Ink opens and closes it and Priya calls the last chorus.

How it was made

The lyric is written as a score, one note per syllable with its pitch and length in beats; the runtime plans the phones onto the notes and sings them. The cues (spoken lines and sung sections, with their voices, seeds and gaps) are listed in a script and placed on a timeline by make_track.py; a doubled chorus is the same score rendered per voice, aligned note by note onto the lead and summed.
