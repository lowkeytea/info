Scylla’s Band

Text-to-speech that runs on the phone or computer it’s installed on. No server, no account, no per-character bill.

Ten voices cover American and British English, Spanish, Italian, French, German and Vietnamese, with four delivery controls: energy, tension, valence and assertiveness. Audio streams as it’s generated, so long text starts playing right away.

  • 10 voices
  • 7 locales
  • 24 kHz audio
  • 4 delivery controls

There’s a Python package and CLI, a C++ library with a C API, and sample apps for iOS and Android. It still trips on the odd word, and a few voices get rough on fast, wide pitch jumps. It speaks in its own ten voices only and can’t clone anyone else’s.

About

Lowkey Tea LLC is an independent software developer based in Washington State.

We build consumer apps and games for iOS and Android. A lot of our work goes into machine learning that runs on the device itself: models small enough to ship inside an app and fast enough to run on a phone, so features can work offline and what you type or say doesn’t have to leave your device.

Scylla’s Band is the first of those models we’ve released. It’s free to use under the Apache 2.0 license, including in commercial products.
